SuraMed is an AI radiology company built for the Arab world, focused on developing advanced clinical imaging tools tailored to hospitals, clinics, and radiology centres across Saudi Arabia and the broader Arab 
region. The platform will offer AI-assisted medical image analysis, multi-modality support, bilingual (Arabic/English) clinical reporting. 
SuraMed addresses a critical gap in the region's healthcare system, where growing demand for medical imaging, high rates of diabetes and cardiovascular disease, and 
radiologist shortages in rural areas make AI-powered diagnostics an urgent priority aligned with Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030.
